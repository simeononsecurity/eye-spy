How to Flash

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop/laptop.
  2. Select your hardware from the dropdown above.
  3. Connect your device via USB.
  4. Click Install and select the serial port from the browser dialog.
  5. Wait ~30 seconds — the device reboots automatically when done.
  6. Open a serial monitor at 115200 baud to see detections live.

What It Detects

Eye Spy passively monitors 802.11 probe requests and BLE advertisements for signals associated with Flock Safety infrastructure. No data is transmitted.

  • 35 Flock Safety Wi-Fi OUIs (field-verified MAC prefixes)
  • 6 contract-manufacturer OUIs (Liteon / USI)
  • SoundThinking / ShotSpotter infrastructure (d4:11:d6)
  • BLE device names: FS Ext Battery, Penguin, Flock, Pigvision, Raven
  • BLE manufacturer ID 0x09C8 (XUNTONG / Flock Safety)
  • Raven camera GATT service UUIDs (8 UUIDs)
