Eye Spy

Surveillance Camera & Tracker Detector — Browser-based firmware installer

1
Connect Your Device
Plug your ESP32 board into this computer via USB. Use Chrome or Edge on desktop — Web Serial is not supported in Firefox or Safari.
No drivers are needed on modern macOS / Windows 11.
2
Select Firmware
Choose the variant that matches your hardware.
3
Flash Firmware
Click Install, select your device's serial port, and wait ~60 s for flashing to complete.

After flashing, open the Dashboard tab above to see live suspicion score & detections.

What It Detects

BLE Scan WiFi Probe OUI Fingerprint SSID Heuristics MAC Correlation

Builds a cumulative suspicion score to detect Axon body-cams, Flock Safety cameras, AirTags, drones, and other surveillance devices.
Output: [eyespy] +5 (Axon-cam) score=10  ·  [eyespy] status score=10 ALERT phase=BLE tracked=3
